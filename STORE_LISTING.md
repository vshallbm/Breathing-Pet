## Long description

**Breath Break** interrupts your scroll with a one-minute guided breathing session — a tiny cat (or other companion) leads you through box breathing or coherent breathing, right in your browser.

**How it works**

Install the extension, pick the sites where you tend to doom-scroll, and Breath Break will pop up at a cadence that adapts to how much you've been browsing. Complete the one-minute session, rate your mood, and get back to it.

**Features**

- Guided box breathing (4-4-4-4) or coherent breathing (5-5)
- Adaptive frequency — starts gentle, learns your scroll patterns
- Multiple companions: Cat (free), Sleepy Dog (unlocked after 7 sessions), and more with Plus
- Four themes: Light, Dark, Sepia, Forest
- Optional ambient audio: chimes, lo-fi rain, forest sounds, Tibetan bowl, calm voice
- Streak tracking and mood check-in after each session
- Focus mode: a corner mascot runs a 25/5 Pomodoro timer and triggers breaks at rest
- Body-double toggle for accountability during deep work
- Fully private — no accounts, no servers, no telemetry. All data stays on your device.
- Accessible: ARIA live regions, focus trap, keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+B to dismiss), reduced-motion support

**Your data**

Nothing leaves your browser. Settings and streaks are stored in `chrome.storage.sync` (synced across your Chrome devices). No analytics. No ads. No tracking.
